# FL47012 (Kawneer IR 521/521T/521UT): learned rules

These rules apply **only** to reviews of FL47012. Do not carry them over to other approvals. Precedence: these rules > Kawneer manufacturer rules > general rules. The sealed approval documents outrank all of them. Entry format: see SKILL.md, "Learned rules".

### R-001  5" mullions only on request (aesthetic)
- Scope: FL47012 (IR 521/521T/521UT) only, field and pre-glazed
- Rule: 5" mullions (5M1-5M7, sheets 9 and 12) are typically not used on IR 521. Select from the standard mullions (M1-M5, sheet 7/10) and expansion mullions (E1-E5, sheets 8/11). Offer 5" mullions only when the user specifically asks for them for aesthetic reasons. If a layout only works with a 5" mullion, say that the standard and expansion mullions don't reach it and give their limits. Mention the 5" option as available-on-request; don't recommend it.
- Why / source: user instruction, 2026-10-19 ("5\" mullions are typically not used for IR 521. Only if specifically requested for aesthetic reasons")
- Applies when: any IR 521 mullion selection or maximum-spacing question
- Status: active
