# FL47880 (Kawneer IR entrance doors): learned rules

These rules apply **only** to reviews of FL47880 and its products (.1–.7). Do not carry them over to other approvals.
Precedence: these rules > Kawneer manufacturer rules > general rules. The sealed approval documents outrank all of them.
A rule that applies to only one sub-product must say so in its Scope line (for example "Scope: FL47880.6 only").
Entry format: see SKILL.md, "Learned rules".

### R-001  Missile levels: LMI = Level D; only FL47880.6 is Level E
- Scope: FL47880 (all products .1–.7)
- Rule: Read "LMI" (large missile impact) on these drawings as large missile Level D. FL47880.6 (IR350/500 HW, KAW044) is the only product with Level E rows; every other product is Level D for large missile. Where a drawing also states small missile (SMI / "large and small impact rated": .1, .3, .5, .7), the product also covers small missile Level A. .2 and .4 say only "impact", so they are recorded as Level D only.
- Why / source: user instruction 2026-10-21 ("LMI is Level D, only one of the FPA's is Level E"). Missile wording per drawing: KAW046/KAW041/KAW043 "LMI/SMI"; KAW047 "large and small impact rated"; KAW042/KAW045 "(impact)" only; KAW044 per-row D/E. Small missile = Level A follows the skill's existing convention (FL47012 "Small Missile A"), not this instruction.
- Applies when: choosing the FL47880 sub-product for a project's missile level; a Level E project can use only FL47880.6, and only its rows marked E.
- Status: superseded by general rule R-004 (LMI = Level D, references/general-rules.md) and FL47880 R-002 (approval-specific part), 2026-10-21

### R-002  Only FL47880.6 is Level E; missile levels per sub-product
- Scope: FL47880 (all products .1–.7)
- Rule: FL47880.6 (IR350/500 HW, KAW044) is the only product with Level E rows. Every other product is large missile Level D (general rule R-004). Where a drawing also states small missile (SMI / "large and small impact rated": .1, .3, .5, .7), the product also covers small missile Level A. .2 and .4 say only "impact", so they are recorded as Level D only.
- Why / source: user instruction 2026-10-21 ("only one of the FPA's is Level E"). Missile wording per drawing: KAW046/KAW041/KAW043 "LMI/SMI"; KAW047 "large and small impact rated"; KAW042/KAW045 "(impact)" only; KAW044 per-row D/E. Small missile = Level A: SMI is the TAS 201 2 g steel ball at 130 ft/s, the same test as ASTM E1996 Level A (references/missile-levels.md).
- Applies when: choosing the FL47880 sub-product for a project's missile level; a Level E project can use only FL47880.6, and only its rows marked E.
- Status: active
